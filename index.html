About

Welcome to my page! I am an NSERC Postdoctoral Fellow hosted by Professor Radu Boț at the University of Vienna.

In May 2025, I received my PhD in mathematics at the University of British Columbia under the supervision of Professor Xianfu Wang.

Research

My research lies at the intersection of monotone operator theory, variational analysis, and continuous optimization, with a focus on the convergence theory of algorithms for “non-problems”, which refer to challenging large-scale optimization problems in modern applications.

Recent Interests

  • Variational Convexity: Theory and Applications.
  • Proximal Algorithms: Nonconvex and/or Non-Euclidean Variants.
